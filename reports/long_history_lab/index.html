What this means in plain English

This version held up reasonably well on fresh unseen data.

Best practice-round setup Compression Breakout Retest with 48-bar setup, trading both, during all hours, risking about 0.50% per trade.
Why that looked promising It stayed positive across all 3 practice rounds and also survived tougher cost assumptions.
Why we are still cautious The final test finally made money, but the practice-round winner and the final winner were different, so we still need one more round of confirmation.

What should we do next?

Keep this family, then test tighter market-condition rules before risking real money.

Keep Compression Breakout with 48-bar setup is the first setup here that passed the final unseen-data check with a positive result.
Change Add smarter market-structure rules on top of compression breakout with 48-bar setup instead of endlessly tweaking trade size and settings.
Avoid Do not treat the current bot as live-trading ready yet.

How We Keep Ourselves Honest

We now ignore flashy low-sample ideas unless they trade enough times to be worth taking seriously.

Minimum validation trades 20 trades across the validation rounds.
Why this matters A setup that looks great on only a handful of trades can be luck, not a real edge.

How The Ranking Worked

Step 2: Tougher costs We re-tested with harsher trading-cost assumptions to see which ideas were fragile.
Step 3: Final unseen period We then checked the finalists on fresh data they had not been ranked on.
